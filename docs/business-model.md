# AfyaMind Business Model

## Summary
AfyaMind is a mental wellness platform for patients, community health workers, clinics, NGOs, and county health programs. It combines self-guided support, CHW coordination, reminders, AI assistance, and follow-up tracking in one workflow.

## Target Customers
- Individuals who want affordable mental wellness support and guided self-care.
- Clinics and therapists who need lightweight patient follow-up and engagement tools.
- NGOs and public health programs that support communities through CHWs.
- Employers and schools that want basic wellness support for their members.

## Revenue Streams
- Freemium patient plan:
  Basic journaling, check-ins, reminders, and limited AI support are free.
- Premium patient subscription:
  Unlock richer AI guidance, advanced progress insights, personalized wellness plans, and certificate/history exports.
- CHW and clinic subscriptions:
  Pay per CHW, per clinic, or per active patient for dashboard, caseload, messaging, and reporting features.
- NGO and county partnerships:
  Annual or multi-year licenses for community programs, training, and deployment support.
- SMS and outreach bundles:
  Charge for reminder campaigns, motivation SMS, and follow-up messaging usage.

## Pricing Direction
- Free tier for patient onboarding and habit-building.
- Low monthly premium plan for individuals.
- Team pricing for CHWs, clinics, and community programs.
- Custom enterprise pricing for NGOs, counties, universities, and employers.

## Why It Can Win
- Built for local care workflows, not just generic meditation.
- Supports both self-service users and human follow-up teams.
- Works well for low-resource environments with SMS and simple operational flows.
- Can start with direct users, then expand into institutional partnerships.
